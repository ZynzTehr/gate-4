# Pricing, reasoning, and walk-away floor

Project: Automatic booking and schedule helper for Reyna's Ride Repair
Prepared by: Jorge Bucio

---

## Build fee and running cost

**Build fee.** $650 one-time flat fee.

**Running cost.** About $25 a month, paid directly to the service providers.

**Spoken price line.** "It's $650 to build, and about $25 a month to run."

---

## Reasoning for the $650 build fee

### 1. Honest estimate of 8 base hours

Last week I built and tested an automated message triage and spam filter workflow. Because I already built the core logic, I can break the work for Reyna down into four concrete parts:

**Build it.** 3.5 hours. Write the intake rules for bike repairs, set up the instant text and social reply flow, add the spam filter, and connect the daily repair counter so Saturdays stop accepting bookings once Reyna hits her limit.

**Test it.** 1.5 hours. Send real test texts and social messages covering normal repair requests, vague messages that need a follow-up question, spam messages, and requests for a Saturday that is already full.

**Set it up on her side.** 2.0 hours. Connect Reyna's actual shop phone number and social page accounts, set up the shared phone calendar, and place the quick-entry bookmark on the device at the front counter.

**Show them how to use it.** 1.0 hour. Run a thirty-minute walkthrough at the counter with Reyna and her cousin, and hand over a one-page printed reference sheet to keep by the till.

### 2. Adding half again for 12 hours total

8 hours plus 4 hours equals 12 hours.

Connecting a small shop's live phone number and social page always takes longer than the code itself. Password resets, two-factor codes on the shop phone, and adjusting the reply wording after her cousin tries it at the counter will eat up those four hours. Counting all 12 hours upfront reflects the actual time the job will take.

### 3. Choosing an hourly rate of $55

From the standard $40 to $75 an hour starting range, I chose $55 an hour based on two facts:

**Who the client is.** Reyna runs a two-person bike shop and is already worried about whether she can afford help. Charging $75 an hour would push the build price to $900, which is too steep for a neighborhood repair shop that has already been burned by software once.

**My confidence in delivering it.** I spent eleven years in truck shop and dispatch operations and just built a working triage automation last week. I am not guessing how to build this, so pricing at the $40 bottom of the range would undervalue work I know how to deliver cleanly.

### 4. The math and clean rounding

12 hours multiplied by $55 an hour equals $660. I rounded that to a clean $650 to build.

---

## Reasoning for the $25 monthly running cost

I separated the running cost from my build fee so Reyna knows what she pays me once versus what the tools cost each month:

**Shared phone calendar.** $0 a month using a standard Google Calendar account shared between Reyna's phone and the counter device.

**Message sorting and spam check.** Under $1 a month. A neighborhood bike shop gets a few dozen messages a week, which stays inside the free or pennies-per-month tier.

**Automation and text connection.** About $20 to $25 a month for the starter automation and text messaging plan that connects her shop phone and social page to the calendar.

---

## My floor

**My walk-away floor is $475.**

Below $475, the 12 hours of setup, live testing, and in-person counter training at the shop are not worth taking on. Writing $475 down today, before talking to Reyna, means I will not negotiate against myself or drop my price out of nerves during the silence.

**How I will hold the line if she pushes back on price.** First, I will state "$650 to build, and about $25 a month to run" and stop talking. If she says that is more than she expected, I will answer with the value to her shop instead of discounting.

**If her budget genuinely cannot reach $650.** I will not do the 12-hour scope for less money. If I choose to come down to $500, I will remove the social page setup from Phase 1 and build text message booking plus the shared Saturday calendar first. That cuts three hours of account setup and testing while keeping the job above my $475 floor.
