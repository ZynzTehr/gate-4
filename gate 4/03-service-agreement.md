# Service agreement

Client: Reyna's Ride Repair
Builder: Jorge Bucio

---

## 1. Scope of work

I will build, test, and set up an automatic repair booking and schedule helper for Reyna's Ride Repair covering these six items:

**Instant customer replies.** Connect one shop phone number for text messages and one shop social page so any customer who messages the shop receives an automatic reply in under a minute asking what repair they need and what day they want to drop off the bike.

**Spam filtering.** Block junk texts and sales messages automatically so only real repair inquiries go onto your schedule.

**Shared repair schedule with daily limits.** Set up one shared phone calendar for Reyna and the front counter that counts booked jobs per day and stops offering a day once it hits your repair limit.

**Counter booking link for your cousin.** Set up a simple one-screen booking page on the counter device so walk-in and phone call bookings take fifteen seconds to log and update the same schedule.

**Counter walkthrough and printed guide.** Run one thirty-minute live training session at the shop for Reyna and her cousin, and provide a one-page printed cheat sheet to keep by the till.

**Two-week adjustment window.** Provide fourteen days of support after launch to adjust the reply wording or daily repair limits at no extra charge.

## 2. What is not included

To keep this project simple, fast, and affordable, this agreement does not include:

- Cash register, point-of-sale, invoicing, or credit card payment tools.
- Bike parts inventory tracking or supplier ordering.
- Building or updating a shop website or writing social media posts.
- Automatic price quotes for custom builds or complex repairs. The system will flag those messages and text them to Reyna to price directly.
- Monthly service fees for the texting and automation tools, which run about $25 a month and are paid directly to the service providers.

## 3. Changes to scope

Any work or extra feature not listed in Section 1 is not included in this price. If you want to change the scope or add something new later, we will agree on the change and the price in writing before I do the work.

## 4. Price and payment terms

**Build price.** $650 total.

**Deposit.** $325 is due when we sign this agreement before work begins.

**Final payment.** $325 is due upon completion, once the system is connected to your shop phone and social page, tested on live messages, and walked through with both you and your cousin at the counter.

**Monthly running cost.** About $25 a month, billed directly by the service provider.

## 5. Timeline

Total time from kickoff to handoff is five business days.

**Day 1.** Twenty-minute check-in at the shop to confirm your daily repair limits, shop hours, and phone and social page logins.

**Days 2 and 3.** Build and test the automatic replies, spam filter, and Saturday booking limits on a test line so your live shop messages are not interrupted.

**Day 4.** Connect the system to your live shop phone and social page and run test bookings together.

**Day 5.** Thirty-minute walkthrough at the counter with you and your cousin. The system goes live and your fourteen-day adjustment window starts.
